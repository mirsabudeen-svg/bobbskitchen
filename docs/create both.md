## Key Insights from the Visualizations

### DPI Requirements Chart

- **600 DPI required** for: Keychain, Phone Case (tiny surfaces need high detail)
- **300 DPI sufficient** for: All other products (T-shirt, Laptop skin, Helmet sticker, etc.)


### Durability Chart

- **Highest durability (score 4-5):** T-shirt, Keychain, Phone Case, Helmet Sticker, Water Bottle (2-5+ years)
- **Medium durability (score 3):** Accessories, Laptop Skin, Bag Sticker (1-3 years)
- **Lowest durability (score 2):** Flip-Flops, Shoes (6-18 months due to flex/wear)


### Radar Chart Insights

- **Laptop Skin** has the largest print area but lowest flex resistance needed
- **Flip-Flops** need highest flex resistance but lowest detail level
- **Phone Case** excels in color flexibility and detail level but has smallest print area
- **T-Shirt** is balanced across all dimensions
