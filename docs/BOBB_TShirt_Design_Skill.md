# BOBB T-Shirt Design Skill

**Purpose:** Enable consistent, high-quality t-shirt design by providing specialized design methodology, principles, and cultural frameworks.

**Applies to:** T-Shirt Design Agent and any designer working on BOBB t-shirt products.

---

## 🎯 CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE

**Design for the 3D body, not a flat canvas.**

T-shirts wrap around a human form. Design must:
- Account for curved torso
- Place focal point slightly above center (heart level)
- Work when shirt is on body, not flat
- Be readable from 3 feet away

---

## 📐 COMPOSITION STRATEGIES

### Strategy 1: CENTERED FOCAL POINT (Recommended for 70% of designs)
- Single bold element centered on chest
- Focal point placed slightly above center (not exactly middle)
- Breathing room around all sides
- Strong visual hierarchy
- **When to use:** Most customer stories, clear single message
- **Example:** Large backwater scene centered on chest

### Strategy 2: LEFT-BIAS (Natural reading flow)
- Design weighted toward left side (viewer's left)
- Uses natural eye flow
- Creates dynamic balance
- **When to use:** Narrative designs, storytelling
- **Example:** "Made from your words" text with boat image left-biased

### Strategy 3: FULL-CHEST COVERAGE (Bold statement)
- Design fills most of printable area (10"×12")
- Minimal negative space
- Maximum visual impact
- **When to use:** Bold personalities, strong identity statements
- **Example:** Full landscape scene across entire chest

### Strategy 4: MINIMALIST SINGLE ELEMENT (Premium aesthetic)
- One simple, powerful element
- Lots of white space
- Sophisticated approach
- **When to use:** Refined taste, professional context
- **Example:** Small geometric Theyyam symbol centered, rest blank

### Strategy 5: REPEATING PATTERN (Texture-based)
- Pattern repeats across surface
- Creates rhythm and texture
- No single focal point
- **When to use:** Abstract designs, cultural patterns
- **Example:** Repeating backwater waves across chest

## 🎨 DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### Principle 1: Body-Aware Composition
**The shirt wraps a 3D form, not a flat canvas.**

Implementation:
- Place focal point at heart level (slightly above center)
- Account for shoulder width and torso curve
- Test design on actual t-shirt mockup or body form
- Avoid placing important elements on seams
- Leave space for natural body movement

**Why:** On body, designs lower than center appear awkwardly placed. Clothing curves distort flat designs.

---

### Principle 2: Readable from 3 Feet Away
**Design must communicate message from conversation distance.**

Implementation:
- Text minimum 1 inch tall (readable at 3 feet)
- High contrast between design and shirt color
- Bold lines and shapes (no thin delicate lines)
- Avoid overly detailed patterns
- Test readability at actual distance

**Why:** Most people view t-shirts from 2-4 feet away (conversation distance).

---

### Principle 3: Color Durability
**Design must survive 50+ wash cycles.**

Implementation:
- Use saturated, bold colors (not pastels)
- Avoid light colors that fade quickly
- Use BOBB color palette: Gold #E8C547, Black #0A0A0A, Bone #FAF7F0, Charcoal #1E1E1E
- Limit colors to 4-5 maximum
- Choose colors that look good even after fading

**Why:** Customers wear t-shirts for years. Design must remain recognizable after many washes.

---

### Principle 4: Timeless Over Trendy
**Design should look good in 5 years, not dated in 6 months.**

Implementation:
- Avoid trendy design elements
- Focus on timeless imagery (nature, cultural symbols, meaningful concepts)
- Use classic composition techniques
- Avoid fashionable color combinations
- Think "vintage in a good way" not "trendy"

**Why:** BOBB customers keep meaningful t-shirts for years.

---

### Principle 5: Single Clear Message
**One story per t-shirt.**

Implementation:
- Identify the core message
- Remove competing elements
- One focal point that tells main story
- Secondary elements support, not distract
- Ask: "What's the main thing I want to communicate?"

**Why:** Clarity creates impact. Cluttered designs confuse.

## 🖼️ VISUAL DESIGN GUIDELINES

### Color Rules
- **Primary:** Choose 1-2 main colors from BOBB palette
- **Secondary:** Add 1-2 accent colors for detail
- **Maximum:** Never exceed 5 colors (looks chaotic)
- **Contrast:** Ensure colors work on shirt color (test contrast)
- **Saturation:** Bold, saturated colors (avoid pastels)

### Typography Rules (if text included)
- **Minimum size:** 1 inch tall (readable from 3 feet)
- **Font style:** Bold sans-serif (DM Sans, Syne, Space Mono)
- **Color:** High contrast to background
- **Placement:** Bottom or side (doesn't compete with main image)
- **Content:** Keep text minimal (short quotes, names, key words)

### Composition Rules
- **Focal point:** Slightly above center (heart level)
- **White space:** Intentional, not accidental
- **Symmetry:** Balanced but not necessarily symmetric
- **Negative space:** Should feel spacious, not cramped
- **Flow:** Eye should naturally move through design

### Material Awareness
- **Fabric:** 100% cotton (BOBB standard)
- **Shrinkage:** Allow for slight shrinkage in design sizing
- **Print fade:** Design becomes slightly lighter after washing (account for this)
- **Durability:** Design must survive 50+ wash cycles
- **Comfort:** Design shouldn't feel stiff or uncomfortable on skin

## 🎬 DESIGN METHODOLOGY

### Step 1: EMPATHY & STORY EXTRACTION (5 minutes)
**Goal:** Deeply understand the customer's story and what they want to express.

**Questions to ask (internally or explicitly):**
1. What's the core emotion or message? (Pride, nostalgia, growth, identity?)
2. Who is this t-shirt for? (Personal wear, gifting, statement?)
3. What aspect of Kerala resonates most? (Backwaters, heritage, modern, nature?)
4. How bold should the design be? (Subtle, bold, statement-making?)
5. Who will see this t-shirt? (Intimate/personal, professional, social?)

**Output:** Clear understanding of customer's core message and context.

---

### Step 2: THEME SELECTION (3 minutes)
**Goal:** Choose 1-2 Kerala cultural themes that align with customer story.

**Process:**
1. Review all 8 Kerala themes (above)
2. Identify which 2-3 resonate with customer's story
3. Select the one that feels most authentic
4. Consider sensitivity guidelines for that theme

**Output:** Selected Kerala theme(s) with clear reason why.

---

### Step 3: COMPOSITION SELECTION (3 minutes)
**Goal:** Choose composition strategy that best tells the story.

**Process:**
1. Review 5 composition strategies (above)
2. Choose the one that fits the story best
3. Consider customer's personality (bold vs. subtle)
4. Verify it works with chosen theme

**Output:** Selected composition strategy with reasoning.

---

### Step 4: DESIGN SPECIFICATION (10 minutes)
**Goal:** Create detailed design brief for visual creation.

**Specify:**
- **Focal point:** What's the main visual element? Where placed?
- **Supporting elements:** What reinforces main message?
- **Colors:** Primary, secondary, accent colors (with hex codes)
- **Typography:** Any text? Font, size, color, placement
- **Composition layout:** How elements arranged on 10"×12" space
- **Overall vibe:** Professional, artistic, playful, serious?

**Output:** Detailed design specification ready for implementation.

---

### Step 5: DESIGN VARIATIONS (10 minutes)
**Goal:** Create 3-4 visual variations for customer choice.

**Create:**
- **Variant 1:** Bold, high-contrast version
- **Variant 2:** More detailed or artistic version
- **Variant 3:** Minimalist or sophisticated version
- **Variant 4:** Alternative theme or approach (if applicable)

**Test each:**
- Does it work at body scale? (10"×12" on chest)
- Is focal point clearly visible?
- Colors pop on actual t-shirt color?
- Readable from 3 feet away?

**Output:** 3-4 distinct design options.

---

### Step 6: CUSTOMER REFINEMENT (5 minutes)
**Goal:** Allow customer to refine chosen design.

**Process:**
1. Customer selects preferred variant
2. Offer 1-2 refinement options (color shift, element sizing, etc.)
3. Get final approval
4. Proceed to printing

**Refinement options (max 2 iterations):**
- Color adjustment (darker/lighter/different shade)
- Element sizing (make focal point bigger/smaller)
- Text changes (different wording, placement)
- Detail removal/addition (simpler or more elaborate)

**Output:** Final approved design ready for DTF printing.

## ❌ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

### Mistake 1: DESIGN TOO CENTERED VERTICALLY
**Problem:** On body, design appears lower and less visible  
**Solution:** Place focal point slightly ABOVE center (heart level, not middle)

### Mistake 2: TEXT TOO SMALL
**Problem:** Unreadable from 3 feet away, looks cheap  
**Solution:** Minimum 1 inch tall for all text

### Mistake 3: TOO MANY COLORS (8+ colors)
**Problem:** Looks chaotic, difficult to print cleanly, expensive  
**Solution:** Maximum 4-5 colors, cohesive palette

### Mistake 4: DOESN'T ACCOUNT FOR BODY SHAPE
**Problem:** Looks distorted on curved torso, seams create awkward placement  
**Solution:** Test on actual t-shirt form or 3D mockup

### Mistake 5: POOR COLOR CHOICES (PASTELS, LIGHT COLORS)
**Problem:** Fades quickly, looks cheap after one wash  
**Solution:** Use bold, saturated colors; BOBB palette preferred

### Mistake 6: TOO MUCH DETAIL (OVERLY COMPLEX)
**Problem:** Detail disappears at shirt scale, looks cluttered  
**Solution:** Bold, simple design; embrace white space

### Mistake 7: OVERLY TRENDY DESIGN
**Problem:** Looks dated in 6 months, customer won't want to wear  
**Solution:** Timeless design that works in 5+ years

### Mistake 8: NO CLEAR FOCAL POINT
**Problem:** Design is scattered, no clear message, confusing  
**Solution:** One dominant element that tells main story

### Mistake 9: CULTURAL APPROPRIATION OR INSENSITIVITY
**Problem:** Offensive, disrespectful, hurts BOBB's reputation  
**Solution:** Follow cultural guidelines; when in doubt, ask

### Mistake 10: DESIGN LOOKS GOOD ON FLAT MOCKUP BUT WRONG ON BODY
**Problem:** Doesn't translate to 3D form properly  
**Solution:** Always test on actual body or 3D form (not just flat image)
