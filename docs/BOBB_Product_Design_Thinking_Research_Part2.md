# 4️⃣ LAPTOP SKIN

## Overview
**Premium Product | Full-Surface Coverage | High Visibility**

Laptop skins are full-coverage designs on a flat surface (13"×9" typical), visible constantly during work. They're essentially "portable art" that travels everywhere.

---

### 🎯 DESIGN THINKING RESEARCH

#### 4.1 Technical Constraints

```yaml
Print Area:
  Standard 13": 13" × 9" (330mm × 230mm)
  Standard 15": 15" × 10" (380mm × 255mm)
  Standard 14": 14" × 9.5" (355mm × 241mm)
  Aspect Ratio: Landscape (approximately 1.4:1)
  Coverage: Full laptop lid
  
Print Method: Vinyl Printing + Lamination
  Process: Full-color UV print on vinyl adhesive backing
  Resolution: 300 DPI minimum
  Color: Full RGB (unlimited colors)
  Coverage: 100% of surface
  
Material: Premium Vinyl with Adhesive Backing
  Durability: 3-5 years with normal laptop use
  Adhesion: Strong but removable (no residue)
  Finish: Matte (prevents glare, hides fingerprints)
  Thickness: 0.2-0.3mm
  
Hard-to-Print Areas:
  Edges: Last 0.25 inch on all sides (wrap-around area)
  Camera area: Usually left clear on modern laptops
  Hinges: Design doesn't extend to folding area
  Logo area: Depends on whether logo stays visible

Application Requirements:
  Precision: ±1mm alignment critical
  Smoothness: Must be bubble-free, seamless application
  Protection: Needs cleaning before application
  Durability: Must survive opening/closing 1000+ times
```

# 5️⃣ MOBILE PHONE CASES

## Overview
**Intimate Product | 3D Wrapping | High Handling**

Phone cases are the most touched products (100+ times daily), must work in 3D space, and are highly personal. Design must wrap around curves and edges while remaining visible and durable.

---

### 🎯 DESIGN THINKING RESEARCH

#### 5.1 Technical Constraints

```yaml
Print Area:
  Typical: 5" × 8" front surface (127mm × 203mm)
  Edges: Must wrap 0.5-1" around sides
  Back: Often printed (usually same as front or different)
  Challenge: 3D wrapping on curves, corners, edges
```
