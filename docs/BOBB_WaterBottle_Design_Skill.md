# BOBB Water Bottle Design Skill

**Purpose:** Enable consistent water bottle design through cylindrical wrapping expertise and curve compensation.

**Agent Type:** WaterBottleDesignAgent  
**Applies to:** All water bottle design tasks

---

## 🎯 CORE PRINCIPLE

**Dance with the curve, don't fight it.**

Water bottles are cylindrical. Design wraps 360°. Embracing the curve creates magic.

---

## 📐 THE WATER BOTTLE CHALLENGE

```
Print Area:         4" × 6" (on curved cylinder surface)
Form Factor:        Cylindrical (not flat)
Wrapping:           360° around bottle
Horizontal Lines:   Must remain level despite curve
Viewing:            Design visible from any angle
Durability:         2-3 years with daily use
Material:           Plastic or steel bottle
Print Method:       UV printing
Curve Distortion:   15-20% visual stretch
Complexity:         Medium-high (3D wrapping challenge)
```

The entire design must work seamlessly when wrapped around a cylinder.

## 🎨 COMPOSITION STRATEGIES

### Strategy 1: HORIZONTAL BANDS (Recommended - 35% of designs)

**Description:**
- Design composed of repeating horizontal bands
- Works naturally on cylindrical surface
- Left and right edges naturally connect
- No wrapping complexity

**When to use:**
- Horizontal elements (water layers, sky layers, horizon)
- Repeating pattern
- Striped or banded imagery
- Simplest wrapping challenge

**Example:** "Water layers with boats at different heights, banded horizontally"

**Strengths:**
- Works naturally on cylinder
- Edges connect seamlessly
- No distortion concern
- Clear, bold composition

**Challenges:**
- Limited vertical variation
- Must fill 4"×6" space well

### Strategy 2: VERTICAL CENTERPIECE (20% of designs)

- Landscape or scene-based
- Customer identity clear on front

**Strengths:**

- Easy composition

## 🎭 DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### Principle 1: Cylindrical Wrapping
**Design must wrap seamlessly around curved surface.**

Implementation:
- Consider the bottle as 4" wide × 6" tall rectangle... that wraps
- Left edge MUST connect to right edge (if wrapping)
- Plan seam position carefully (usually back of bottle)
- Account for curve distortion in composition

Testing:
- Print mockup and physically wrap around cylinder
- Do edges connect seamlessly or is there a visible seam?
- Does design look intentional when wrapped?
- Any awkward misalignment?

**Why:** Wrapped design must appear seamless or it looks broken.

---

### Principle 2: Level Horizontals
**Horizontal lines must remain level despite cylinder curve.**

Specification:
- All horizon lines must be perfectly level
- Water/sky transitions must be straight
- Banding must be even
- Testing on actual cylinder is ESSENTIAL

Testing:
- Wrap design on cylinder
- View horizontals from all angles
- Do they appear level when cylinder rotates?
- Or do they appear crooked?
- Adjust if needed

**Why:** Horizontal distortion looks unintentional and wrong.

---

### Principle 3: Curve Compensation
**Visual distortion occurs when design wraps 3D surface.**

Challenge:
- Flat design on flat surface = accurate
- Flat design wrapped on cylinder = 15-20% visual stretch
- Shapes distort, proportions shift
- Plan for this distortion

Strategy:
- Account for 15-20% stretch in width
- Shapes that appear narrow on flat become normal when wrapped
- Vertical elements can stretch slightly
- Horizontal elements must resist distortion

Testing:
- Create flat design
- Wrap on cylinder
- Measure actual appearance vs. intended
- Adjust proportions to compensate

**Why:** Without accounting for distortion, design looks wrong on bottle.

---

### Principle 4: All-Around Visibility
**Design must be interesting from every viewing angle.**

Implementation:
- No "wrong" angle to view bottle
- Front is primary, but back/sides matter
- 360° coherence
- Each side should be beautiful

Testing:
- Rotate cylinder 360°
- Does design feel complete from every angle?
- Any awkward transitions?
- Interesting from all views?
- Back looks as intentional as front?

**Why:** Bottles rotate. Design must work from all angles.

---

### Principle 5: Color Vibrance on Material
**UV printing on plastic/steel requires bold color choices.**

Implementation:
- Bold, saturated colors (pastels disappear)
- Limited color palette (3-4 colors max)
- High contrast for visibility
- Colors chosen for bottle material

Testing:
- Do colors pop on bottle material?
- Bold enough to be visible?
- Coordinated and professional?
- Work from distance?

**Why:** Bottle material affects color appearance. Colors must be chosen for that context.

## 🎬 DESIGN METHODOLOGY

### STEP 1: STORY & COMPOSITION SELECTION (2 min)
Define the story and choose composition strategy (5 options above)

### STEP 2: EDGE PLANNING (2 min)
If wrapping: plan left/right edge connection carefully

### STEP 3: HORIZONTAL VERIFICATION (2 min)
Ensure all horizontal lines are perfectly level

### STEP 4: CURVE DISTORTION ACCOUNTING (2 min)
Plan for 15-20% visual stretch on cylindrical form

### STEP 5: COLOR SPECIFICATION (2 min)
Choose bold, saturated colors (3-4 max)

### STEP 6: MOCKUP WRAPPING (3 min)
Print and wrap design on actual cylinder bottle

### STEP 7: ANGLE TESTING (2 min)
Test from all viewing angles

### STEP 8: ADJUSTMENT (2 min)
Adjust horizontals, edges, or proportions as needed

### STEP 9: FINAL APPROVAL (1 min)
Approve wrapped version ready for UV printing

## ❌ COMMON MISTAKES

1. **Flat design thinking** → Distorts on cylinder
2. **Edges don't connect** → Looks broken
3. **Horizontal lines crooked** → Looks unintentional
4. **No curve compensation** → Wrong proportions
5. **Complex detail** → Visual noise on curve
6. **Ignores 360° visibility** → Back looks incomplete
7. **Weak colors** → Invisible on bottle
8. **No mockup testing** → Wrong on actual bottle
